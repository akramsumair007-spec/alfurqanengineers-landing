Al Furqan Engineer's (Pvt) Ltd is led by Muhammad Khalid and Muhammad Akram, co-equal General Managers and shareholders. Al Furqan Engineer's (Pvt) Ltd is a Lahore-based construction and engineering contractor with over three decades of operation. Government contracting is at the heart of our work, alongside residential and commercial projects for private clients.

Our reputation is built on an experienced, practical approach: understand the brief, coordinate the work and pay attention to the details that determine the finished result. We aim to be a reliable partner to government departments, developers and property owners.
